# Companions, vacancies and dismissal

The party has five positions. Death and departure leave an empty position instead of erasing the person. **View Party** shows current travelers and vacancies; **Show past travelers** reveals their retained health, social and personal histories. Both columns scroll independently.

## Finding someone

Click a vacancy or use **Options > Look for companions**. Recruitment is available at staffed towns, forts, other populated trading stops and passing-trader camps. An empty stretch of trail has nobody to search for. Closed services and people refusing the party because of its crimes also block recruitment.

A search takes one day, including meals, weather, illness and wage effects. Each place permits one search per journey, with an authored 65% chance of a candidate. Reopening, returning or declining does not reroll the offer. The preview shows the person's appearance, heritage, temperament/values, background, specials, flaw, starting trust and wage requirement. Accept or decline explicitly. Inviting someone costs no further day.

Generated strangers begin with neutral peer affinity and the catalog's stranger trust toward the leader. They can subsequently form friendships, rivalries and, when enabled, romances. Appearance and heritage remain descriptive. Mercenaries require their existing background/special-based minimum wage; the first payment falls due relative to the actual hire day. Free companions use trust without a wage contract.

Recruits bring one clothing set and 15 lb of personal gear, but no food, money or ammunition. They start at 85 health. Daily food demand, cargo, carrying, wages, disease and social rules include them. These supplies and recruitment rates are provisional game choices, not measured historical averages.

## Asking someone to leave

Select an active companion in **View Party > Ask to leave**. This works while camped or stopped where ordinary services are available. It opens a review before committing. It is unavailable during unresolved encounters, hunting or crossing sequences. The leader cannot dismiss themself.

Partners leave together, and the preview names them and shows each person's food, clothing and personal gear. The ordinary departure supply allowance applies. Dismissal costs no day or ammunition and creates no violent crime. Remaining close companions receive a base trust loss of four; others lose one, subject to their usual trust modifiers. It does not trigger the assault system's injury, death or witness consequences. Wages already owed stay in the archived contract; pay them before dismissal if desired.

## Saves and identity

Traveler ids are permanent and append-only. Replacements occupy physical vacancies but never inherit a dead person's graves, crime identity, contract or memories. New actors and social pairs are registered without resetting existing bonds. Search results, accepted/declined offers, vacant positions and dismissal confirmations save and load. Old five-person saves initialize their original positions without inventing recruits.

This bounded first implementation allows five active travelers and up to 32 total traveler records per journey. It records up to 128 searched sites. It does not provide returning departed travelers or recruitment negotiations. Combined survival balance and human playtesting remain outstanding; older balance campaigns do not validate the new replacement mechanic.
