# Short human playtest after the simulation pass

Use the current game, Classic mode. The large simulation sample does not measure whether the interface makes danger understandable or whether aiming and interruptions feel good.

## First 10-15 minutes

1. Start a farmer in May with the suggested six-ox outfit and ordinary unpaid companions. Keep the seed in your notes. Inspect the opening cost and remaining cash.
2. Pause and open **Options > Provisions / Cargo**. Confirm you can distinguish food units, physical meat weight, wagon space and the current hunting haul.
3. Hunt once. Check the report: ammunition, physical meat obtained/carried/left, food units recovered and a day spent. Compare with the cargo screen. The live hunting label should show the actual helper limit rather than a fixed100 lb.
4. Preview drying and salt-assisted drying when available. Confirm you understand the day, salt, food loss and storage benefit before committing.
5. Inspect individual travelers when illness/injury occurs. A broken leg removes hauling contribution; ordinary poor health reduces it gradually. Recovered capacity should return as the traveler improves. Wagon space itself should not shrink with human health.
6. Open the wagon-abandonment pack preview, then cancel. Confirm its personal carrying limit and retained gear are clear. Abandonment is an emergency decision; this check does not require actually leaving the wagon.
7. Save, quit and load. Check the date, food, fresh batch age and personal conditions. Continue for a few days and note any confusing health or food interruption.

## Optional second session

- Compare an ordinary store holdup with quiet theft. The loot panel should state whether the wagon is alongside or supplies must be carried away. Unwilling companions and broken legs should not contribute to the carried-loot limit.
- Try a longer journey with steady pace, timely resupply and hunting before the larder is empty. Keep four working oxen when possible; taking food from the working team can turn a shortage into a travel crisis.
- Note whether a warning arrived early enough to change your decision. Report the seed, approximate date/location and the choice you expected to work. A saved journey is particularly useful for diagnosis.

Do not use the archived aggregate arrival percentage as a prediction of your first run: the matrix includes late August departures, fragile parties, payroll obligations, no-hunting and violent strategies, and deliberately cautious scripted travel. Human aiming, judgment and interface pacing remain unmeasured.
