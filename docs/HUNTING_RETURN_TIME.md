# Hunting return time and farmer follow-up

Animal hauling limits are unchanged. A riding outfit that plans to lead home or use a slower heavy load reserves more of its hunting day for the return. The outfit screen shows the remaining search percentage and planned return buffer before setting out. Fewer potential animals are encountered; aiming time, shot rules and carcass yield do not change. The outing still costs one shared day and one meal, with weather, spoilage, health and animal wear applying normally.

The planning calculation is shared by leader and companion outings. A companion on a multi-day trip has already traveled out, so the last hunting day reserves return time without charging the outward journey again. Earlier hunting days keep their normal opportunities. Stranded hunters surviving at their camp do not repeatedly pay a return penalty. Actual pursuit still uses real load, weather, terrain, season and river/fork rules.

## Authored assumptions

The configured fair-weather model uses a nominal 10-hour outing, an 8-mile search radius and daily animal travel allowances converted over eight travel hours. It compares each chosen return with the same outfit under normal riding loads. Actual animal/rider/tack freight and human hauling determine planned movement factors. Search opportunities have a 25% floor so planning does not produce an automatic prohibition in an emergency.

For a fit solo horse with bags, normal riding retains full baseline search. Heavy riding, leading home and heavily loaded leading reserve successively more time. These are game-planning estimates, not measured 1848 hunting hours or a real animal-load recommendation. Local foot/wagon hunts retain their existing baseline; wagon noise still applies separately. A party already traveling at walking pace may incur little additional return-time cost by leading, which is intentional.

Normal outings keep their original game/cover/yield random stream. A separate seeded opportunity roll removes some potential game when the search budget is lower. Companion return-day filtering likewise leaves actual hit rules and ammunition costs intact. Old saved outings retain their stock, supplies, state and RNG on load; future hunting uses the current rules. A genuine pre-change active led-trip fixture verifies migration.

## Farmer study design

The follow-up compares cautious/steady pace and standard/lean starting outfits on the same source and matched worlds. The experimental lean outfit has four oxen and 1,200 food units; clothing, 200 bullets, care/repair kits, three spares, salt and dried produce are retained. It frees $85 at departure but gives up 500 initial staple food units and two spare oxen; it is not a free resource boost or a new default outfit.

The main matrix uses all seven existing hunting strategies, practical equipment costs, three professions, May/July departures and separate six-seed development/validation cohorts. It retains the prior short health-rest, produce, preservation and public-action rules. No prices, starting money, animal hauling, health or general travel speed are retuned.
