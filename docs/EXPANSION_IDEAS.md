# Expansion ideas after horse loads

Spencer's proposed future work, not implementation commitments for this slice:

- Audit disease breadth: measles already exists alongside dysentery, cholera (1849+), typhoid, mountain fever, exhaustion and scurvy. Smallpox is not implemented. Check route/date-specific evidence and overlap with existing contagion/immunity before adding diseases.
- Columbia descent: the existing The Dalles/Cascades branch and portage/rapids risks are playable. Consider an isometric animation, then decide whether a rafting minigame improves the choices.
- Isometric combat: combat currently resolves through choices, ammunition, health, background and risk. A tactical minigame would be a separate design.
- Captured outriders: short custody with an opportunity to intervene remains proposed. Establish locality, authority and 1848 jurisdiction before detention or prison breaks. Do not assume every trading post has a prison.
