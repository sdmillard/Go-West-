# Wage crews and leathercraft: frozen protocol 1

Production base: 581fc4ab64a21122afef880f39ec782adca74b4a. No production tuning.

Confirmatory matrix: seeds 571000-571003, April/May, no livestock/two goats,
free/hired artisan, and off/crew/craft income strategies: 96 journeys, 32 matched
triplets. Four worlds recur across month and setup strata; these are not 96
independent random worlds. Earlier income studies are development evidence only.
Pilot 570900 checks instrumentation; it is excluded. Policy freezes before opening
confirmation outcomes. No subsequent strategy adjustments are permitted.

All cells use the current skilled farmer survival policy, shared horse, 65% seeded
hunting aim, normal pace, standard outfit, no extra money, and five keepsakes.
Companions 1-4 have Carpentry, Leatherworking, Hunting and Caregiving backgrounds;
4 has Haggler. Artisan 2 has Veteran/Loyal and one Proud flaw. Hired cells add
Mercenary and the real market daily quote times seven as an automatic weekly
contract. All customization goes through departure validation. No recruitment.

Off: no earning activities. Crew: existing actual-kill pelt selection and consented
keepsake sales below $10; ordinary buying first; sale-only visits must exceed
estimated visit meals at regional staple prices. Below $20 plus a weekly contract
bill, with one carried party meal, choose maximum gross legal employment crew.
Preserve assigned nurses/patients and reserved livestock carers; respect actual
post/role quotas and health rules. No arbitrary autumn cutoff. One shared workday
and one party meal, regardless of worker count. All wage contracts accrue normally.

Craft: same employment and survival rules. A free qualified companion may replace
rest/hunt-help with leatherwork on already required camp days, after nursing,
preservation, forage and livestock reservations. No dedicated craft-only rest.
Keep one usable input pipeline while cash >=$2 and one meal remains; sell surplus
and gloves through real counters. Actual-kill skin selection may value a deer hide
at four glove pairs' conservative road price rather than rough resale, if the
artisan remains present. This is a strategy assumption, not guaranteed proceeds;
all actual haul displacement, preparation days, failed/unfinished craft and unsold
stock are measured. Other pelt logic retains v2 positive estimated margin and four
meals including known recoverable meat. No extra hunts or free materials.

Outcomes: arrival, original survivors/present, elapsed days, food deficit/deaths,
gross receipts by wages/pelts/keepsakes/craft, actual contract payments/arrears,
calendar workdays versus worker-days, wage-floor uplift, craft input/output stages,
meals/time on earning days, and leftover materials. Gross receipts are not profit.
Compare all matched triplets, including losses, and report free/hired separately.
Shared-arrival timing is secondary and omits unmatched losses explicitly.

Reports carry exact source hashes/rules fingerprint. Runner refuses overwrite,
archives raw JSON and hashes it. Verifier checks matrix/parity/source signatures,
crew receipts and quotas, craft conservation and unique meal-day accounting.
Targeted integration fixtures exercise the full actual hide-to-glove sale chain
and weekly contracts even if a natural journey never finds a viable hide.
