# Recurring local contacts

October27,2026. All seven community visits now have connected, persistent individual dealings. Choose **Visit** under the landmark picture, then **Speak with...**. The contact is the same fictional person on return visits and after saving. Their decisions apply to their household or services, not an entire nation.

## Fort Hall: the provision trader

A Northern Shoshone provision trader offers a one-off day moving packed loads, a clothing-for-provisions barter, or a free refusal. Work pays the current leader's ordinary market wage with a $1 floor. The leader works; other present companions follow their existing camp orders. One day means normal food, weather, illness and payroll. Completed work leads to an agreement on the remaining household provisions. Barter or completed work earns five percent off that household's finite remaining parcels. Work cannot be repeated, and declining means the household arranges the job elsewhere.

Barter consumes one real clothing set and one existing40-unit household parcel, without another day or cash payment. It is a costly emergency food option, not a favorable replacement for cash shopping: clothing can be worth substantially more elsewhere. The same two-parcel stock supports both buying and barter. No replenishing inventory, free new parcel or unlimited job is created.

## Deschutes: the river pilot

A local pilot asks the party to move stock and baggage clear of a working landing. Spend a real unpaid labor day, or pay $1 for help with no extra day. Refuse and insist on that landing, and this pilot declines further assistance. The regular river methods remain available. Reconciliation requires an apology and a real unpaid workday; saved history distinguishes reconciled dealings from an agreement made without the dispute.

After clearing the landing, the pilot can offer a short westbound agreement toward The Dalles. The quote is $2 per wagonload, one load minimum on foot, with one day to arrange it. It includes the pilot's own provisions. Coverage lasts the resulting booked day and the next two days, ending sooner on arrival at The Dalles or a turn east. Turning west again or returning to Deschutes cannot revive a closed agreement. Renewing an expired or closed agreement costs another fee and arrangement day. Adding more wagons than the booked loads invalidates coverage until appropriate terms are arranged.

The same existing0.85 crossing-risk factor applies; guidance and preparation do not stack. The pilot does not prevent drowning, accelerate travel, join a permanent party slot or supply free food. The companion is narrative company on this16-guide-mile stretch; there is no separate guide sprite or independent offscreen movement simulation. A day-only guide booking remains available under the earlier rules when this pilot is willing to deal.

## The Dalles: the Wasco provision seller

A recurring seller offers one job during the authored April–October work window: pack preserved provisions, or repair a storage rack if the leader has a suitable Carpentry background. Both use ordinary quoted wages with a $1 floor. Completing either closes both job offers, grants the same small household-only discount, and leads to an offer of an introduction.

An introduction opens the existing local companion search, with the same65% chance, one day/meals, candidate terms and permanent accept/decline record. It does not create a second roll or guarantee recruitment. A found candidate's panel explains the seller's introduction and their interest in company toward the Willamette Valley. This is personal context, not yet a separate destination/departure system. The current generic fictional name pool remains in use.

## Pawnee provision keeper: a finite supply exchange

In Platte country, the keeper of a household's provisions offers one 40-food-unit parcel for 20 lb of salt. The exchange takes no extra day and removes an actual existing household parcel; it cannot be repeated or completed without the salt. Good dealings earn the same small household-only provision discount. Refusing the bargain leaves ordinary trade open. This is an authored individual offer, not a claim about a fixed Pawnee salt tariff. The nominal outfitting values are $1.20 for 20 lb salt and $2 for 40 food units before local adjustments; the exchange is deliberately finite, and salt also has preservation uses.

## Nez Perce horse owner: terms on the same herd

At Grande Ronde, the owner of the already-listed sale herd offers a real unpaid day handling horses in exchange for five percent off the remaining horses' coin price: $19 instead of the existing $20. The first horse trade still takes its normal visit day. Clothing, ammunition and ox barter quantities remain unchanged. No extra horses, free tack or healing are created; purchases use the existing `herders:grande_ronde|buy_horses` stock. The owner remembers completed work, and it cannot be repeated to stack discounts. Declining keeps ordinary terms open.

## Umatilla garden keeper: seasonal in-kind wages

The household offers one workday gathering/packing produce during its existing June–October supply window. The leader must pass the ordinary work eligibility rules. Their party eats normal meals, weather and payroll advance, and the employer hands over four fresh-produce baskets afterward instead of cash. These are normal physical food/vitamin batches with ordinary spoilage. They do not instantly cure scurvy. The baskets consume one of the household's two listed fresh-produce parcels, so a parcel already sold cannot also pay a worker. The work earns the small household discount but no second job, refill or guaranteed companion. Declining is neutral.

## Cascades load organizer: one labor credit

The organizer offers one unpaid day carrying/organizing loads in exchange for a $1 credit against a future paid portage. The credit applies ONCE to the total bill: one wagon is $3-$1=$2; two wagons are $6-$1=$5. It is not a per-wagon discount or cash wage. The paid portage still takes its own day. Normal self-portage and rafting remain available.

Closing the conversation or saving retains the credit and refreshes the actual portage quote. It is consumed only when the landmark commits to the paid departure, after any departure guards. Inadequate cash does not consume it. Redemption is saved and returning cannot earn a second credit. Helping costs food/time and may be less useful than an ordinary wage job elsewhere; it can provide an option for a party short of cash.

## Historical basis and authored choices

- [Nebraska State Historical Society's Pawnee marker](https://history.nebraska.gov/marker-monday-pawnee/) supports central Nebraska/Pawnee-country context, farming and extended Platte/Republican-country hunts. [Pawnee Nation history](https://pawneenation.org/pawnee-history/) remains a community reference. Neither source supplies the fictional salt bargain's quantities or terms.
- [Nez Perce Tribal Tribune, April2022, digital p9](https://nezperce.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Volume-4-Issue-8.pdf) describes historical horse breeding/use. The owner, handling job and discount are original gameplay, not a reconstruction of a named person or transaction. No modern Nez Perce Horse breeding program is projected into 1848.
- [CTUIR comprehensive plan, digital p30](https://ctuir.org/media/sychezsg/2018updated-2010_comprehensiveplan-webversion.pdf) reproduces an 1847 traveler's description of crops along the Umatilla. It supports a food-growing context, not a guaranteed harvest/work schedule for every family in 1848. The June–October window, four baskets, day costs and individual household are authored.
- [Shoshone-Bannock Tribes](https://www.sbtribes.com/about/) describes distinct Northern Shoshone and Bannock communities, broad ancestral country, seasonal food collection, fishing and buffalo hunts. That context supports preparations and trade around Fort Hall; it does not document this particular trader or job.
- [Warm Springs tribal history](https://warmsprings-nsn.gov/history/) describes Wasco food exchange and regional trade networks, distinct from neighboring Sahaptin-speaking communities. The seller, storage-rack job and seasonal work dates are authored rather than transcribed transactions.
- [NPS Columbia River narrative](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/overlanders-in-the-columbia-river-gorge-1840-1870-a-narrative-history.htm) and [full report](https://www.nps.gov/oreg/learn/historyculture/upload/HRA_Columbia_River_Narrative_History_Final_200917-reduced-size.pdf) document paid local navigation/crossing assistance and portage labor. The three-day Deschutes-to-Dalles agreement and the Cascades labor credit, their prices, provisions arrangement, dispute and reconciliation are fictional gameplay, not recorded1848 contracts or established tariffs.

The broader historical setting includes dispossession, disease and conflict. These individual transactions do not represent comprehensive political relations or cancel those existing circumstances. No invented authentic names, ceremonial dialogue, racial skill bonuses or nation-wide friendliness score are used.

## Implementation and limits

`data/native_encounters.json` contains each contact's immutable identity, finite-state conversations, available actions, text, standing and work season. `simulation/native_contact_story.gd` validates and executes a limited set of action kinds. Serialized choices cannot supply arbitrary rewards. Individual contact state lives in the existing `native_contacts[site].story`: step, actual work day, guide coverage end/load count and permanent closure flag. Local standing is derived from the authored stage, rather than accepting a saved reputation number.

Existing `trail_income.worker_available` supplies the same health, broken-leg, absence and nursing restrictions for ordinary settlement jobs and contact labor. The settlement-work day reserves the actual current leader and releases the worker afterward, preventing double camp jobs. Other companions retain their camp orders. Labor is one opening for the leader; it is not a second multiple-worker settlement menu.
