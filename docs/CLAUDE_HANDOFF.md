Archives' later iron-box reference has no firm1848 installation date; don't depict it as proven. Territorial creationAug1848 did not replace the provisional administration untilMar3,1849. Volunteer militia existed in western Oregon in1848, separately from the U.S. Army: don't conflate Fort Lee/The Dalles, Waiilatpu militia camp or HBC Walla Walla; exact event dates/capabilities remain to verify.
