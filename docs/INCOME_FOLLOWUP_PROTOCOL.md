Version1 is retained unchanged: 32 fresh cases (four shared seeds567000-567003), seven arrivals per arm, and no actual employment or skin preparation. It therefore measured only keepsake sales, despite enabling all three mechanisms. Those results are development evidence, not a successful full exercise of the new earning options.

Before new confirmation data, declare a single follow-up with fresh seeds568000-568003, the same32 cells (income off/combined, standard outfit, April/May, no stock/two goats), and the same production rules b54cbb0 and inherited informed65% strategy. Keep all version1 buying, selling, worker-selection and gross-margin rules. Change only these study decisions:

1. The four-meal safety test for preparing skins includes carried food plus food recoverable from the animal already killed, after reserving the raw hide's haul. Version1 checked only pre-harvest food, although it generally hunted when that stock was nearly empty. Do not manufacture a kill, recovery or extra hunt. The estimate does not foresee future encounters or buyer availability.
2. Remove the study's extra October employment cutoff (and its skin cutoff); actual game availability, weather, winter cost, health, food, wages and work quotas still apply. An arbitrary date should not prevent a needy party considering a lawful job the game offers.

These changes address test coverage and overcautious scheduling, not a discovered production wage/food bug. Do not alter prices, game mechanics or reserve thresholds to improve outcomes.
