# Produce, preservation and outfitting

Fresh fruit/greens and dried produce can be bought and sold through post, trader and emigrant-family counters, and purchased before departure. The catalog uses **baskets**, each representing four pounds of fresh produce and one food-value unit. A fresh basket weighs four pounds; a dried basket weighs 0.7 lb. Opening illustrative prices are $0.32 fresh and $0.40 dried per basket. These are thematic estimates, not recovered 1848 local tariffs. Regional prices, trader temper, family reluctance and the existing Trading background benefit apply at counters; stock and demand are finite. Fresh availability follows the growing season, while dried stock may be present outside it.

Fresh baskets use the existing three-day produce expiry. Dried baskets keep up to ninety days and retain a quarter of the fresh-equivalent vitamin-C contribution per food unit. The retention, weight and shelf-life numbers are explicitly authored parameters pending balance/playtesting; individual fruits and drying methods differ. Neither carrying nor selling produce supplies dietary vitamins. Only actual meals count.

Meals now share enough available produce for the existing vitamin-C serving abstraction, then use ordinary provisions. Additional produce can feed a calorie shortage. This avoids eating a large reserve of dried fruit as bulk calories at the start while leaving the flour untouched. The shared-party deficiency counter remains an abstraction, not a physiological nutrition simulator. A modest twenty-basket test outfit weighs fourteen pounds and costs eight dollars; the game does not force that purchase or increase starting funds.

**Camp > Dry fruit / greens** previews the mass and one work day. Up to eight baskets can be set aside for processing. The party must eat from other provisions during that day; the reserved batch cannot both feed the workers and become dried food. Produce that will already have spoiled cannot be rescued. The process uses no salt, preserves its game food value, reduces mass and vitamin C, and advances ordinary illness, weather, wages and time. This is a bundled camp/fire-drying abstraction, not a claim that open-air drying always finishes in one day. Salt-assisted meat drying remains separate.

Tagged nutrition and expiry survive save/load, cache transfers, discards and exact counter transactions. Selling produce removes that specific type in expiry order. A basket cannot sell the same food twice through produce and ordinary-food rows. Ordinary food trade still values mixed provisions as food units; it cannot create an extra produce reserve. An earlier compatible rules fingerprint (`dd57503977a35f4cc96129e69ccd4cb4e58cbbdaee0bb6eb236f9af3e4106f49`) is preserved in the migration list.

## Historical basis and limits

[Palmer's 1847 emigrant guide](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46906/pg46906-images.html) recommends a bushel of dried fruit for each adult. This is a volume recommendation; it has not been silently converted to a universal dry mass. [Colonial Williamsburg's preservation history](https://research.colonialwilliamsburg.org/DigitalLibrary/view/index.cfm?doc=ResearchReports%5CRR0064.xml) describes sliced apples/peaches dried by sun or hearth and packed into containers. [National Park Service orchard history](https://home.nps.gov/grsm/learn/historyculture/apples.htm) describes sun/fire drying and the reduction in hauling weight. These establish period-compatible methods, not inventories at every Oregon Trail post.

[NIH's vitamin-C reference](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminC-HealthProfessional/) notes losses with heat and prolonged storage. The game's quarter-retention setting is not a measurement inferred from that general statement. Seasonal fresh plants remain valuable even when preserved food is available.

## Verification

`tests/test_produce.gd` covers opening cost/mass reconciliation, fresh/dried nutrients, actual processing days and meals, sale/purchase mass, mixed-cart overselling, expiry, invalid nutrition, saves and caches. Store lists scroll inside fixed panels rather than overlap their totals. `tests/test_current_policy.gd` verifies the balance observer produces the same simulation state and RNG as the unmodified engine and counts actual underfed days. Frozen-source, matched-seed campaign reports evaluate the combined mechanics separately from these regression tests.
