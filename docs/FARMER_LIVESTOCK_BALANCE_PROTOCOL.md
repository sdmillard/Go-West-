# Farmer livestock comparison: declared protocol

Freeze simulation/data and all campaign/observer GDScript before final testing. Do not alter prices, production, survival rules or policies after seeing development or confirmation outcomes. Existing small pilot seeds 301010-301011 are excluded.

## Matrix

Farmer, same $675 starting budget, five ordinary generated starting travelers; May and July. Two existing outfit profiles: standard (six oxen/1,700 food) and lean (four oxen/1,200 food); each includes the same shared horse/saddle/bags and 20 dried-produce units. Steady travel, adaptive rations, actual aimed hunting at 65%, two-meal hunt threshold, mobile outriders, parallel camp jobs, shared-stop fishing. Existing health-rest limits remain bounded. No blanket week-long recovery policy.

Five arms: no livestock; two milk goats; one milk cow; six hens plus 50 lb grain; one cow/one goat/six hens plus 50 lb grain. All purchases reduce existing cash and add actual cargo. No free outfit compensation.

For each livestock arm compare existing mobile decisions with a predeclared stock-aware policy: reserve noncritical helpers for chores, protect nurse/patient and at least one forager, avoid proactively dispatching reserved caretakers; replenish at most 14 days grain when below three days, after normal purchases and retaining $20; emergency slaughter of cow then goat if hungry and short of a meal. Neither policy buys replacement livestock. Both can discard grain to fit walking packs. The no-stock baseline runs once because stock-aware branches are inert without livestock.

Development seeds 311000-311005 and confirmation seeds 321000-321005. Nine arm/policy cells x two months x two outfits x six seeds x two phases = **432 journeys**. Six underlying seeds per phase repeat across month/outfit settings; these are paired scenarios, not 432 independent random worlds. No policy selection/tuning between phases.

## Evidence

Read-only observers count actual livestock production, food eaten/spoiled/removed/left, unmet care, feed shortages, condition/crossing/slaughter losses, ordinary food shortages, purchases, time, scurvy and causes/locations of death. Compare completion and party survival; compare travel time only for paired arrivals. Earlier deaths are not faster trips and shorter exposure can reduce raw shortage counts.
